About

Marco Antonio García

I've spent more than 25 years designing homes, additions, and ADUs across the South Bay and greater Los Angeles, and walking them through city plan check until they're approved.

I'm also a trained actor and theater director, which is where the theatrical side of my work comes from. Years on stage taught me how a space should feel to the people inside it, and I bring that to every project.

My personal affinity of style is Mid-Century Modern: the clean lines and warmth of the tones, the scale, craftsmanship, wonder, and the home feeling—from growing up in one—is what leads me in finding the right design for the project.

Many of my clients come back, and some of their children now hire me for their own homes.

From First Visit to Permit

01

Site visit

I measure what's there and learn how you want to use the space.

02

Design

Layouts and options until the plan works for how you live or do business.

03

Complete plans

A full set of plans, coordinated with structural engineers.

04

Permits

Submittal, plan check corrections, and follow-through until it's approved.

When a project requires a licensed architect's stamp, I work alongside licensed architects of record and structural engineers, so you have one point of contact from start to finish.
